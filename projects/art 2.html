Warehouse controls retrofit

Migrated legacy PLC logic to a modular architecture, cutting mean recovery time after faults by 35%.

Nerd

Describe the legacy controls pain points: monolithic logic, long recovery after faults, limited diagnostics, or change risk.

Approach

  • Decompose the program into reusable modules by equipment zone.
  • Standardize fault handling and operator messaging.
  • Stage cutover with parallel validation against the old system.

Outcome

Capture the 35% recovery-time improvement and any secondary wins (easier changeovers, clearer alarms). Link to architecture diagrams if you have them.
