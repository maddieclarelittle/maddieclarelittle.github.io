Thermal path study for drive cabinet

FEA and bench testing to validate heat rejection under peak load; informed enclosure and fan selection.

Challenge

State the thermal risk: hotspot locations, ambient conditions, derating limits, and what failure looked like if unmanaged.

Approach

  • Build a thermal FEA model of the drive cabinet and airflow path.
  • Correlate with thermocouple data from a peak-load bench test.
  • Trade enclosure vents, fans, and component placement against noise and cost.

Outcome

Summarize the selected cooling solution and how it kept components within spec. Drop in FEA plots or test charts when you have them.
