Autoloader Twist Cage

Designed an addition to an exsisting piece of equipment. Historically the autoloader on the fillet line faulted and jammed frequently. This was caused by the frozen fish sticking together once they were cut with the bandsaw. This twist cage was the first step to solving this problem. This design was placed directly at the output of the band saw to immediately separate the two fish pieces to prevent them from sticking together. The previous design in the machine has the two cut pieces sitting next to each other after being cut but before being physically separated, allowing time for them to stick back together. Learned how to use SolidWorks sheet metal during this project. Completed as a rising junior in 2024.
