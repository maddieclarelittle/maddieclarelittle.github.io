Symbotic project 1

Created a production ready add-on instruction (AOI) for a SICK Lector83x Barcode Reader (BCR) by completing the R&D process from start to finish. Worked with multiple teams at Symbotic and at SICK to learn about the product and the set up proccess. Over the course of 4 weeks commissioned the device, integrated the new device with Symbotic PLCs, programmed the BCR to be able to read both 1D and 2D barcodes, and tell if the quality of the barcode reading is sufficicent to parse the data.
