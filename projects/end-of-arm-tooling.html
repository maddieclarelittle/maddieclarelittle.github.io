End-of-arm tooling redesign

Redesigned gripper geometry and compliance for mixed-SKU handling; reduced drop events in pilot by 48%.

Challenge

Explain SKU variability, grip failures, and any constraints on mass, cycle time, or payload that drove the redesign.

Approach

  • Characterize failure modes from pilot data and high-speed video.
  • Iterate gripper geometry and compliance in CAD and bench fixtures.
  • Validate across a representative SKU set before line install.

Outcome

Document the 48% drop reduction and any tradeoffs (wear, changeover time). Add before/after photos or section views when available.
